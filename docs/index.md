# My app

An example guide, for the template's example page. Replace it with your app's: say what the app is for, in two sentences.

## Post a message

1. Type in **Your message**.
2. Press **Post it**. The message appears at the top of the list, with your name and the time.

A message is at most 500 characters.

## Good to know

- Everyone who can open the app reads every message.
- Messages are kept in memory: they are gone each time the app restarts or is deployed.
- Administrators can remove a message with **Delete**.
